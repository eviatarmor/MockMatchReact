A strong resume is readable by a human in under a minute and parseable by applicant tracking systems (ATS). MockMatch helps you build, score, and tailor that document; this guide is the craft layer on top of [Resume Lab](/docs/documents/resume-lab).

## What “good” means here

- A recruiter can map you to the role without decoding jargon
- Bullets show outcomes, not only responsibilities
- Keywords match the job honestly (no keyword stuffing)
- Formatting stays plain enough for ATS and for PDF export

There is no single universal template that wins every industry. Optimize for the role you want next.

## Structure that scans

Use a clear top-to-bottom order:

1. **Contact and target**: name, links you want them to open, optional headline aimed at the role family
2. **Summary** (optional): three to four lines max; skip if experience already tells the story
3. **Experience**: reverse chronological; company, title, dates, bullets
4. **Projects** (if they strengthen the case): especially for career switches or sparse titles
5. **Education and credentials**
6. **Skills**: honest inventory aligned to the job

Keep layout calm: standard section headings, consistent dates, no multi-column tricks that break ATS parse.

## Write impact bullets

Prefer this shape:

> Action + context + outcome (metric when you have one)

Examples of direction (invent your own real numbers):

- “Cut p95 API latency 35% by rewriting the cache invalidation path”
- “Owned onboarding for a 6-person squad; reduced time-to-first-PR from 10 days to 4”

Avoid duty lists (“Responsible for managing…”) and empty intensifiers. If you lack a metric, use scope: users, revenue band, team size, frequency, or risk removed.

## ATS-aware habits (without gaming)

- Mirror **exact phrases** from the job post when they match real experience
- Expand acronyms once if the post uses the long form
- Prefer standard section titles over clever renames
- Export a clean PDF from MockMatch rather than a heavily designed slide resume

Job-specific fit scoring and fit resume generation live in [Discover](/docs/jobs/discover). Use them after the base resume is coherent, not instead of rewriting weak bullets.

## Workflow in MockMatch

1. **Import** your current PDF or start from a template in Resume Lab.
2. Fix structure and bullets in the editor; use the AI rail for section rewrites when you need a draft, then edit for truth.
3. Check **general health** in Resume Lab.
4. For each priority job, open Discover → fit score / fit resume.
5. Export PDF and apply; track the role in [Applications](/docs/jobs/applications).

## Common failure modes

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Wall of text | Shorter bullets; split multi-idea lines |
| No outcomes | Add scope or result to each strong bullet |
| One resume for every job | Keep a base; tailor with Discover fit docs |
| Design over content | Prefer plain structure and MockMatch export |
| Inflated titles | Accurate titles; strong bullets beat vanity labels |
