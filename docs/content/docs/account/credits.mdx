MockMatch runs a free plan with pay-as-you-go **credits** for AI features that cost money. Card details stay with Stripe when top-up is configured. This page describes the product model without inventing plan names or prices beyond what the app shows.

## Open Billing

In the signed-in app: user menu (avatar) → **Billing**. Tabs cover **Usage**, **Payment**, and **Billing history**.

The navbar also shows remaining credits and entry points such as **Get credits** when available.

## Free plan and credits

- No subscription is required for the free plan
- You buy credits for AI features that consume them
- Usage breakdown in Billing can group categories such as mock interviews, fit resume, fit cover letter, and job fit scores

Exact pack sizes and prices come from the live Billing UI and Stripe products for that environment. Docs will not hardcode commercial packaging that may change.

## What may use credits

Examples of credit-gated work:

- Job fit scores and fit document generation
- Conversation mock interviews
- Some collab or AI rail actions depending on plan and grant

If an action costs credits, the product should make that clear in the UI. MockMatch does not claim unlimited free AI when credits apply.

## Top up

1. Open **Billing** → **Usage**.
2. Choose a credit pack and **Buy**.
3. Complete Stripe Checkout when keys are configured.

If Stripe is not configured (common in local dev), the free plan UI still works and paid top-up is disabled. You may see a message that credit top-ups are not configured on this environment.

## Payment method and history

- Cards are collected and stored by Stripe; MockMatch displays last four digits only
- Full card numbers and CVC are not stored on MockMatch servers
- Billing history lists invoices for top-ups and other Stripe charges when present

## Local development note

Developers sometimes raise free grants (for example a large `FREE_CREDIT_GRANT`) so collab and AI paths are testable without Checkout. That is an environment setting, not a public plan promise.
