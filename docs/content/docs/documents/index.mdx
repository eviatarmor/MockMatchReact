## How documents connect to the rest of the product

- **General health score** lives on resumes in Resume Lab
- **Job-specific ATS fit** and fit document generation live in [Discover](/docs/jobs/discover)
- Practice and readiness use your prep activity; they do not silently rewrite your documents

Craft guidance (structure, bullets, tone) lives under [Guides](/docs/guides).
