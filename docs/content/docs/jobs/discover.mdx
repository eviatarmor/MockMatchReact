Discover is where you browse and evaluate roles. Job cards can show AI summaries and fit scores against your profile or resume. From a job you can generate a fit resume or fit cover letter, then track the role into Applications.

## Open Discover

In the signed-in app: **Applications** area → **Discover** (`/discover`).

## Search and browse

Use search and browse to find openings. When Adzuna (or the configured job source) is available in the environment, results come from that source. Empty or limited results often mean the job provider is not configured for that environment.

## Summaries and fit scores

Job cards may show:

- An AI **summary** of the role
- A **fit score** versus your candidate profile or resume

These signals help you decide whether to track, generate tailored materials, or skip. They are decision aids, not guarantees of interview outcomes.

Fit-related AI actions may use [credits](/docs/account/credits).

## Generate fit documents

1. Open a job in Discover.
2. Use fit scoring if you need a job-specific read on a resume.
3. Generate a **fit resume** or **fit cover letter** when the action is available.
4. Open the generated document in Resume Lab or Cover Letters to edit before you apply.

General resume health still lives in [Resume Lab](/docs/documents/resume-lab). Job-specific ATS fit lives here.

## Track into Applications

From the job workflow, track a role so it appears on [Applications](/docs/jobs/applications). Mark applied when you have submitted; that path can also seed interview questions into the global question bank once per job (with semantic dedupe).
