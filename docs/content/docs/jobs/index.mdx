## How jobs connect to documents and practice

- Fit scoring and fit resume / cover letter generation start from a job in Discover
- **Import Job** and **mark applied** can auto-generate interview questions into the shared [Question Bank](/docs/practice/question-bank)
- Active applications show up in readiness-style signals under [Insights](/docs/insights)
