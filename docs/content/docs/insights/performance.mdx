Performance shows how interview practice has trended over time: scores, session volume, time practiced, and movement by domain.

## Open Performance

In the signed-in app: **Insights** area → **Performance** (`/performance`).

## What you see

Typical surfaces include:

- Summary stats (average score, sessions, time practiced)
- **Score trend** over recent weeks
- **By domain** breakdown with movement versus the last period
- Strengths and related narrative when the UI surfaces them

Time range controls (for example last 30 / 90 / 180 days, or all time) let you zoom the charts.

## How to use it with Readiness

- **Performance**: what happened in practice over time
- **Readiness**: what to do next given the whole prep loop

If a domain is flat or falling, schedule a focused simulation or bank sequence in that domain rather than only reviewing the chart. Export is available when the UI offers it.
