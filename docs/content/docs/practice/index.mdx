## How practice connects to the loop

- Import Job and mark applied seed the question bank
- Custom questions let you author private items and deploy them to your own bank
- Session outcomes feed [Performance](/docs/insights/performance) and readiness-style next steps
- Live collab on IDE-style rooms follows the same owner-must-stay-in-room share model as documents
