Interview Recorder is the surface for connecting real interviews so you can capture transcription and insights after the fact. It lives in the Automation area of the nav.

## Open Interview Recorder

In the signed-in app: **Automation** area → **Interview Recorder** (`/interview-recorder`).

## What to expect

Use this page when you want product-guided capture around a live interview rather than a MockMatch simulation. Depth of connect, transcription, and insight pipelines continues to roll out; treat the page as the product entry point and follow on-screen setup.

For mock practice without a real interviewer, use [Simulations](/docs/practice/simulations) conversation tracks instead.

## Related practice

- Conversation simulations for rehearsals before the real call
- [Performance](/docs/insights/performance) for trends from scored practice
- [Question Bank](/docs/practice/question-bank) for targeted drills after you notice gaps
